# TNT extension 1.5 🧨
*TNT extension adds some new and explosive tnt tools*

# Features✨

➡️ get all items via ```/function tnt:give_tnt```
➡️ Or the recipes
➡️ Or ```/loot give [player] loot tnt:[item]```

## **Infinite TNT**🧨

![infinite tnt](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/155d4b62e76b48279e5761034ce4637aa1bc8dd9_0.webp)
- hold rightclick to use
- summon 20 tnt per second
- Explosion level **2/4**
➡️```/loot give @s loot tnt:infinite_tnt```


## **Infinite TNT minecart**🚂
![Infinite TNT minecart](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/baf13706a77db2c0b3f4be087feef5eeff626f73_0.webp)

- hold rightclick to use
- summon 20 tnt minecarts per second
- Explosion level **2/4**
➡️```/loot give @s loot tnt:infinite_tnt_minecart```
**Tip💡: Use it on the ground and hold rightclick a longer time and move**

## **TNT Blaster**🔫

![TNT Blaster](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/6934a7fe5c175495a86724607a4f8aaf33abb6fa_0.webp)

- Rightclick to charge🔫
- Rightclick again to shoot🎆
- works like a crossbow with explosive arrows, multishot 255 and quick charge 255
- Does also work underwater🐟
- Explosion level **3/4**
➡️```/loot give @s loot tnt:tnt_blaster```
## **Super TNT**🎆

![Super TNT](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/feaa59695951c446fa47c86e0a3242cd8a3161e7_0.webp)

- Click one time to use (5s cooldown⏱️)
- summon 1001 TNT minecarts🚂
  - let them explode like the infinite TNT minecart
- The explosion takes like 3min🕰️
- Explosion level **4/4**
➡️```/loot give @s loot tnt:super_tnt```
## **TNT Laser Gun🔦**

![TNT laser gun](https://i.imgur.com/u6C9LIL.png)

- Rightclick to charge🔫
- Rightclick again to shoot🎆
- Best Texture and modell (Thanks to [Foxeddev](https://foxeddev.vercel.app/) and [ALIEN aka cristalight_](https://modrinth.com/user/cristalight_))✨
- Inspired by [KiwiJoe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ksjn7h1oEZ0)🥝
- Explosion level **1/4**
➡️```/loot give @s loot tnt:tnt_laser_gun```
